# Incident Response Policy

## Severity levels

SEV-1 incidents cause a complete customer-facing outage or confirmed data loss. The on-call engineer must acknowledge a SEV-1 alert within 5 minutes.

SEV-2 incidents cause significant degradation but do not completely prevent customers from using the service. They must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.

## Communication

For SEV-1 incidents, the incident commander posts a customer update every 30 minutes. An internal incident channel must also be created.

## Post-incident review

A written review must be completed within five business days of resolving a SEV-1 or SEV-2 incident.
